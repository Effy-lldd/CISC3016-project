Fed Rate Decision Impact on USD/CNY Exchange Rate

Reuters · Published: 2026-04-29 14:00 UTC
Full text of the news here... The Federal Reserve's Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) announced a 25 basis point increase in the federal funds rate, raising it to a range of 5.25% to 5.50%. This decision comes as the Fed aims to combat persistent inflation in the US economy.

For the USD/CNY currency pair, this rate hike has immediately pushed the exchange rate up by 0.15%, reaching 7.2210 at the time of announcement. Market analysts believe that the rate differential between the US and China will widen further, supporting the US Dollar in the short term.

However, the People's Bank of China (PBOC) has signaled that it may take measures to stabilize the Yuan, including adjusting the daily midpoint fix and intervening in the foreign exchange market if necessary.
